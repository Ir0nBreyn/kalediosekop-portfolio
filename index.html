Halo, I'm Dio!

My name is Ardhiya Febrian Rachman, you can call me by Dio, from Indonesia. I'm a Digital Product Designer with 7 years of experience building websites, landing pages, CMS, ERP, SaaS, eCommerce, and custom mobile applications across industries spanning Transportation, Banking, Energy, Healthcare, FMCG, Automotive, eCommerce, Sports, NGO.

I finished my studies as a Software Engineer, then shifted into Graphic Design, working on social media and branding. That background gives me a working understanding of how developers think in logic and frameworks, and how branding shapes the feel of a product. I combine both to design products that feel personified, not just functional.
